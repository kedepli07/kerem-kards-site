Questions

Does it work on every phone?

It works on iPhones from the last several years and almost every Android phone. Customers whose phone can't tap can scan the QR code instead.

Is this allowed by Google?

Yes. The card simply opens your review page. We never offer rewards for reviews and the card goes in front of every customer, not just the happy ones, which is how Google wants reviews collected.

Do I need an app or a subscription?

No. You pay once and the cards work for good. The monthly report is optional.

What do you need from me?

Just your business name and address, and your Instagram handle if you want the QR code. We find your Google review link for you.

How do I pay?

After you order, we text you to confirm the details. You pay by Venmo, Zelle or card, and your cards arrive within 48 hours.
